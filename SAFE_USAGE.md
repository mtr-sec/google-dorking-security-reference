# Safe Use & Responsible Learning

**Maintainer:** [MTR-SEC](https://github.com/mtr-sec)

## Before searching

1. Use a domain you own, administer or have explicit written permission to assess.
2. Confirm the permitted domain, subdomains, dates and activities.
3. Keep learning examples focused on intentionally public pages and documents.
4. Do not attempt authentication, bypass controls or access restricted files.
5. Do not download, retain, copy or share information that appears private or sensitive.

## If sensitive information appears unexpectedly

- Stop reviewing the result.
- Do not open, download or redistribute the material.
- Do not publish the URL or contents.
- Notify the website owner through an official security or privacy contact.
- If you administer the site, secure the source first, then consider appropriate search-engine removal procedures.

## Important distinctions

- `robots.txt` is a crawler instruction, not an access-control mechanism.
- `noindex` can affect search indexing, but does not make a public URL private.
- A hidden URL or obscure filename is not authentication.
- Search results are incomplete and can be stale.

## Good learning practice

- Keep `example.com` in examples.
- Study operators using public documentation, brochures and policy pages.
- Practise remediation in a local lab or a deliberately designed training environment.
- Keep notes free of personal data, credentials, tokens and private URLs.
- Respect privacy, applicable law, terms of service and written authorisation.
